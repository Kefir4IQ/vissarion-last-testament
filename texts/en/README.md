# English source library

This directory maps English publications to the seven Russian source books in [`../ru`](../ru/README.md). The source of truth for citations and passage numbering remains the Russian PDFs. The English texts listed below are available to read at the linked pages; they are **not stored in this repository**. The project initiator reports permission from the translator to republish, but the Booksie pages themselves have separate copying terms. A copy supplied directly by the translator can be added here with its provenance and version recorded.
